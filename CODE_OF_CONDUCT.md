# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, and keep discussions focused on making the skills better. Harassment or personal attacks of any kind are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that violate this code.
