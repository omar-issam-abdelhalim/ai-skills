# Scan and score

## The categories

AI news is wider than model launches. Scan all of these; a funding round or a chip story often has a better angle than the tenth post about the same model.

| Category | What counts | Example queries (append the current month/year or "this week") |
|---|---|---|
| Models | New frontier/open models, major version updates, benchmark releases | `new AI model release`, `LLM launch this week`, `open weights model released` |
| Research papers | arXiv papers with traction, lab research posts, surprising results | `arXiv AI paper trending`, `Hugging Face daily papers`, `AI research breakthrough this week` |
| Infrastructure & compute | Data centers, GPU/TPU/custom chips, inference cost, energy, cloud deals | `AI data center announcement`, `AI chip news`, `GPU supply AI`, `inference cost drop` |
| Funding & M&A | Startup rounds, valuations, acquisitions, IPOs | `AI startup raises Series`, `AI acquisition announced`, `AI valuation round` |
| Products & agents | Agent launches, developer tools, major product features, pricing changes | `AI agent launch`, `AI coding tool release`, `new AI feature announced` |
| Policy & regulation | Laws, export controls, court cases, safety frameworks | `AI regulation news`, `AI export controls`, `AI Act update`, `AI lawsuit ruling` |
| Open source | Major repos, open model families, tooling | `open source AI release`, `GitHub trending AI` |
| Essays & analysis | Influential posts by researchers/founders, big-lab strategy docs | `AI essay researcher`, `AI industry analysis` |
| Hardware & inventions | Robotics, devices, novel hardware, science applications | `AI robotics breakthrough`, `AI hardware device launch`, `AI scientific discovery` |

## Search discipline

- Minimum 10 searches, at least 6 categories, one query per category (combined queries return shallow results).
- Queries short (2–6 words). Include the current year or "this week" / "today". Never use last year's date.
- For each promising hit, confirm the publish date and fetch the primary source if the snippet is thin.
- Prefer original sources: company blogs, arXiv, official filings, government sites. Treat aggregators and social posts as leads, not evidence.
- Window: last 72 hours preferred, 7 days maximum. An older item qualifies only if something new happened to it this week (new data, a rebuttal, a follow-up).

## Scoring rubric (out of 16)

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Recency** | > 7 days | 4–7 days | 2–3 days | < 48 h |
| **Significance** | Minor update | Notable in its niche | Moves an industry segment | Changes how the field works or competes |
| **Audience fit** (AI builders, students, founders, researchers) | Irrelevant to them | Nice to know | Affects what they build or learn | Directly changes their decisions |
| **Angle potential** | Nothing beyond the headline | One modest insight | A clear non-obvious story | A contrarian or systems-level insight others will miss |
| **Visual/data potential** (max 2) | No numbers | Some numbers | Strong numbers that chart well | |
| **Brand fit** (max 2): systems, infra, economics, agents, strategy | Off-brand | Adjacent | Core | |

**Saturation adjustment (−2 to 0):** −2 if every AI page is already posting the same take and there is no distinct angle; 0 if the angle is genuinely differentiated. A saturated topic with a strong unique angle can still win.

Score ≥ 12: strong. 9–11: workable. < 9: weak; say so.

## What makes a good angle

The angle is the reason someone saves the post. Look for:

- **The hidden metric**: the number the headline ignores (cost per task vs price per token; revenue per GPU; tokens per answer).
- **The honest read**: a big claim that's partly true; show which part.
- **The second-order effect**: what this changes for builders, startups, pricing, jobs, or competitors.
- **The pattern**: how this fits a trend across the last few months (only if you can source the earlier points).
- **The builder takeaway**: a decision the reader can make differently tomorrow.

Red flags for a topic: only one source, only a tweet, numbers you can't verify, pure rumor, or a story whose only angle is "wow".
