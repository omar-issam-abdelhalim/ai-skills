# Publish kit

## Caption (English)

Structure, 120–220 words:

1. **Line 1 = the hook**, restated for the feed (only ~125 characters show before "more"). No emoji, no "🚨".
2. **The key numbers** in 2–3 short lines.
3. **"But that's not the real story."** Then the thesis in 2–3 sentences.
4. **Why it matters for builders**: one concrete sentence.
5. **What's inside**: one sentence teasing the honest-read and so-what slides.
6. **A real question**: specific, answerable from experience, not a false binary. Good: "Do you know the cost per task of your AI workflow?" Bad: "Coding agents or automation?"
7. **3–5 hashtags**, specific over generic: e.g. #AIEngineering #AIAgents #LLM + 1–2 topic tags (the company or field involved, e.g. #AIInfrastructure). Never 10+ hashtags.

## First comment

Post from the user's account right after publishing:

> Source: [official source name], [domain/path]. [Figures are [company]-reported.] [Second source if used.]

Links in captions aren't clickable; the comment carries the sources and protects credibility when someone asks.

## Posting time

Audience and timezone come from `brand.md`. The defaults below assume a Middle East audience plus some global followers (e.g. Cairo time); shift them to the audience's local evening.

- **Best default windows**: weekdays 8–10 PM local (peak local activity; for Cairo this overlaps US early afternoon ET). Second choice: 1–3 PM local.
- **Avoid**: after ~11 PM and before ~9 AM local, because the first hour of engagement decides reach and the audience is asleep.
- **LinkedIn**: weekday mornings and lunchtime local (8–10 AM, 12–1 PM) usually beat evenings.
- **Freshness**: news posts should land within ~24–36 h of the event. Analysis posts can land after the first wave of headline posts and stand out more for it. If the event happened late in the evening, the next day's evening slot usually beats posting immediately.
- Always add: "Check Insights → Total followers → Most active times; your own data beats these defaults."
- Give one specific recommendation (day + hour) with a 2–3 line reason tied to the current time and the event's timing. Get the current time from the environment.

## Music

- Instrumental only; lyrics compete with reading.
- Give 3 options: one cinematic/building, one synthwave/electronic, one minimal/ambient. One line each on why it fits this post's mood.
- Names and artists only. Never quote lyrics.
- Instagram only (skip for LinkedIn). Note it's secondary: if a track isn't in the user's Instagram library, any instrumental ambient/lo-fi track works.

Starter pool (rotate; don't repeat the same pick every run):
- Cinematic: Hans Zimmer – Time; Hans Zimmer – Cornfield Chase; Ólafur Arnalds – Saman; Max Richter – On the Nature of Daylight
- Electronic/synthwave: HOME – Resonance; Tycho – Awake; Carbon Based Lifeforms – Photosynthesis; Kavinsky – Protovision
- Minimal/ambient: The xx – Intro; Brian Eno – An Ending (Ascent); Nils Frahm – Says; Jon Hopkins – Immunity

## First-hour checklist

- Reply to every comment in the first hour with substance, not "thanks!".
- Share to Story with a poll or question sticker tied to the post's question.
- Send to 3–5 people who'd genuinely engage (not spam groups).
